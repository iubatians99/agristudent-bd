CGPA Planner

Want a good CGPA? This tool tells you what SGPA you need in each next semester to reach it. Add your past results, pick a target, and see the answer on a simple chart.

  • Free
  • Works on phone
  • Takes 1 minute

How to use it

1

Add your results

Choose the semester you are in now. Then type your SGPA for each past semester (from 0 to 4, like 3.90). Press "Lock results & continue".

2

Pick your target CGPA

Move the slider or tap a button like 3.80. Three small cards will show: Locked CGPA (your CGPA now), Target (your goal), and Need SGPA (the average SGPA you need in the next semesters).

3

Look at the chart

Each bar is one semester. A dark bar is a result you locked. A green bar is a result you expect. A dashed empty bar is what you need in that semester. The orange dotted line is your target.

4

Try your own numbers

Type the SGPA you hope to get in the next semesters. Leave a box empty to see what you need there. The big box at the bottom shows your CGPA and a short message.

Good to know

  • The tool counts 12 semesters. Every semester has the same weight.
  • Your numbers stay in your own browser. We do not send them anywhere.
  • To change your results, press the Edit button.
